Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the act and practice of navigating all online spaces safely, effectively, and responsibly.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law is a set of rules that outlines how people must and must not do while using the internet. Digital Law also applies to uses of technology.

What is AUP?

AUP is an acronym for Acceptable Use Policy, and it is a set of rules made by a school, business, or generally any organization that regulates how you may and may not use online services and networks.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's work without giving proper credit.

Regardless of the intention, it is still plagiarism.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is pretty self-explanatory: it is any action that misrepresents a student's learning.

An example would be cheating.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

AI-generated content must ONLY be used when allowed, and it must be used responsibly.
