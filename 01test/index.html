Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the act and practice of navigating all online spaces safely, effectively, and responsibly.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law is a set of rules that outlines how people must and must not use the internet, the content available on the internet, and all technology.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy, and it is a set of rules made by a school, business, or generally any organization that regulates how you may and may not use online services and networks.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's work without giving proper credit.

Regardless of the intention, whether it was done on purpose or on accident, it is still plagiarism.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is pretty self-explanatory: it is any action that misrepresents a student's learning.

Examples would be cheating/plagiarism, and otherwise lying in schoolwork.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

AI-generated content must only be used when allowed, and it must be used responsibly.
