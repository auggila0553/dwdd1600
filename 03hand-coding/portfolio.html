(Un)Holy Wrath, 2025. Mixed media on bristol.
Artist Statement: Something about a false angel showing its true colors and revealing its malicious and wrathful nature has always fascinated me. In particular, I was inspired by the analog horror series The Mandela Catalogue, where the story follows an apocalyptic invasion of demonic shapeshifters. I chose to work with the color yellow as I not only was drawn to it, but it also carries multiple meanings in Christian art. It's symbolized with gold and light, but it can also be associated with betrayal and envy due to paintings of Jesus's disciples that included Judas Iscariot, as he was usually depicted wearing yellow.
There's the saying that angels often make themselves look scary to ward off evil, but is that really the case with this angel? Is this snarl a mask of an angel, or is this the true face of a devil?
Mother Nature's Embrace, 2025. Mixed media on bristol.
Artist Statement: I've always been someone who's loved the idea of nature personified into some sort of deity. I've had moments where I've changed and lost faith in certain higher powers, but I feel that my belief in the natural world has not been shaken, even if it has taken on different names. Every time I'm sitting in nature and I feel the breeze blow by, I feel comforted. I wanted to express that comfort by depicting humanity in the hands of Mother Nature, being held and watched over as humanity rests peacefully. I used acryla-gouache, oil pastels, watercolor, and ink.
To be in Mother Nature's Embrace is to be protected by tall trees, to be nourished by the plentiful water, to be able to rest on green grass. To be in Mother Nature's Embrace is to be at peace and rest.
Solemn, 2026. Mixed media on canvas.
Artist Statement: Kynan Valdus is a beloved character of mine that I've had since 2018, but it was only recently that I have started using him to depict scenes and ideas of justice, judgment, sin, and morality, as those ideas are crucial to his character and his story. However, here, I wanted to focus more on showing his personality. His eyes are closed and his head is facing down in a pensive manner. He wears his armor as he is framed by the patterns of his own tiara. I used acrylic paint, modeling paste, and air-dry clay.
