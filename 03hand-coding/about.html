About

Background

August Melendez (He/They) grew up in Springville, Utah with three siblings and two cats. They've always had an affinity for visual arts, but it wasn't until they were ten years old that they started taking art more seriously. Upon graduating high school, they attended Utah Valley University in 2025 to study graphic design. They plan to graduate in 2028.

They started their artistic journey drawing their favorite characters from shows they watched in their childhood; one highlighted show being My Little Pony. When they were ten, they picked up a book on how to draw manga characters and began pushing their skills to their limits. As they grew older, they built their own characters and worlds.

Beyond just fictional worlds, they also used art as a means of venting. They especially expressed their struggles with depression through oil pastels, charcoal, and pencils.

Where They Are Now

They are actively pursuing a degree in graphic design in college! They're still making fanart of their favorite characters from shows and games, and they are still making OC art today. You'll likely find them working on their next project, gaming, or doomscrolling.
