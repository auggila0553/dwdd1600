Who Were the Signers?

Famous Signers:

Thomas Jefferson
Thomas Jefferson was one of the delegates of Virginia, the main author for the Declaration of Independence, and later the third President of the United States. He promoted religious freedom and universal rights, although ironically held over 600 people in slavery.
John Adams
John Adams was a representative of Massachusetts and the second President of the United States. He was one of the most influential and important people at the time to fight for the freedoms of the colonies.
Benjamin Franklin
Benjamin Franklin was one of the Founding Fathers of the United States. He served as a diplomat to France, and he was also given credit for inventing pieces of technology like the lightning rod.
Robert R. Livingston
Robert R. Livingston was a lawyer, diplomat for France, and a delegate from New York. He's most famous for negotiating the Louisiana Purchase. He was also known as "The Chancellor" after the New York legal office he held for around 25 years.
Roger Sherman
Roger Sherman was a lawyer and another one of the Founding Fathers of the United States. He represented Connecticut, and he was the only person to sign all four great papers: the Continental Association, the Declaration of Independence, the Articles of Confederation, and the Constitution.
