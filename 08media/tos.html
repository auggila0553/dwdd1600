Terms of Service

Thank you for considering me for your art commission! Before we can begin discussing your vision in detail, please read and agree to the following terms. By commissioning me, you have agreed to the terms that have been presented.


General Terms

  1. You MUST be 18 years of age or older in order to commission me. I do not and will not accept commission requests from minors.
  2. I ask that you send me reference images and/or detailed descriptions of what you're looking for. This is the best way for you to get what you want out of your art commission.
  3. Please do not ask me to imitate the style of other artists; I will not do it.
  4. Estimated completion time will depend on the complexity and difficulty of the commission. It will also depend on my queue size (generally, three days minimum to two months maximum is my timeframe).
  • Please be aware that art is not my full-time job! I am in college, so there may be delays with the commission. If it is time sensitive, please let me know beforehand so we can discuss if the timeframe for the commission is feasible. I will let you know if any delays arise.

Copyright and Usage

  1. All commissioned artwork must be used for personal use only. No commercial use is allowed. I reserve the right to my artwork and the right to post it to my social media. If you want your commission to be private, please let me know.
  2. You are not permitted to claim the artwork as your own. Do not remove watermarks/signatures.
  3. You are not permitted to use my work for AI training and/or NFTs. You will be blocked and blacklisted.
  4. You are permitted to share the commissioned artwork where you wish, but I ask that you credit me.

Payment + Cancellations/Refunds

  1. I take payment through PayPal, Venmo, and CashApp. All prices are listed in USD.
  2. I have thr right to cancel or turn down a commission for any reason.
  3. 50% of the total payment is required upfront before I begin! The other 50% must be paid after the sketch/lineart phase.
  4. Full refunds are available only before sketch/lineart phase. During the sketch/lineart phase, down to 50% of the total price will be refunded. After the sketch/lineart phase, NO refunds will be available. However, if I am unable to complete the commission regardless of where I am (as in, how much progress I've made), I will issue a full refund.

Revisions and Edits

  • Revisions and edits are only possible during the sketching, lineart, and the coloring phases. Clients are allowed up to THREE free revisions. Once sketches, lineart, and colors are approved, no further free revisions will be accepted unless a mistake was made on my part. Any changes requested after the coloring phase will incur an additional fee (this includes entirely new elements such as pose changes, additional limbs, etc etc.)
